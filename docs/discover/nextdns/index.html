NextDNS

The NextDNS integration is used to integrate with NextDNS, a DNS service that protects you from security threats, blocks advertisements and trackers on websites and in apps, and provides a safe and supervised internet for kids on all devices and on all networks. The integration lets you monitor NextDNS statistics and control its configuration.
