Google Nest

Bring Nest events and controls into Home Assistant

Connect supported Google Nest thermostats, cameras, and doorbells through Google's Smart Device Management API. Setup requires a Google Cloud project, a Nest Device Access project with a current US$5 registration fee, and a consumer Google account; Google Workspace accounts are not supported.
