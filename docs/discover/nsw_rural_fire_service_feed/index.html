NSW Rural Fire Service Incidents

The NSW Rural Fire Service Incidents integration lets you integrate a GeoJSON feed provided by the NSW Rural Fire Service with information about bush fires, grass fires, hazard reductions and more. It retrieves incidents from a feed and shows information of those incidents filtered by distance to Home Assistant's location.
