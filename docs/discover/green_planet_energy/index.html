Green Planet Energy

The Green Planet Energy integration provides real-time electricity pricing data from Green Planet Energy, a German renewable energy provider. It fetches 15-minute electricity prices and exposes various sensors for energy monitoring and optimization. Use it to shift your power consumption to cheaper hours and make the most of a dynamic electricity tariff.
