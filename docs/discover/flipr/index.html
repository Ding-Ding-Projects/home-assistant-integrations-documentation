Flipr

Go flipr company sells smart pool monitor and management devices. The Flipr and Flipr Hub devices publish data to the cloud via Wi-Fi and SigFox. This integration gives you access on Home Assistant to the information measured by your Flipr with the same data as the vendor's smartphone application. This integration gives you also access to the Flipr Hub to control your pool equipments like pump, heater, and light.
