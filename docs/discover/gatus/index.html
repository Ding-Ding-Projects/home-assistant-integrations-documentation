Gatus

The Gatus integration connects Home Assistant with your Gatus monitoring instance. Gatus is a developer-oriented health dashboard that lets you monitor your services using HTTP, ICMP, TCP, and DNS queries, and evaluate results based on conditions like status codes, response times, certificate expiration, and response bodies.
