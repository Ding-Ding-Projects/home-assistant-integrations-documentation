KlikAanKlikUit

The KlikAanKlikUit integration lets you control self-learning KlikAanKlikUit 433.92 MHz RF devices from Home Assistant. Home Assistant sends commands directly to your devices through a radio frequency remote adapter, such as an ESPHome device with a 433.92 MHz transmitter. The integration does not connect to KlikAanKlikUit hubs, like the ICS-2000, or to the KlikAanKlikUit app.
