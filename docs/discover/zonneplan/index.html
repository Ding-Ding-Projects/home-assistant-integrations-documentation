Zonneplan

The Zonneplan integration lets you retrieve electricity and gas price information from Zonneplan, a Dutch provider of dynamic energy contracts and energy services, including home batteries and EV charge points. This integration currently provides electricity and gas price entities, your electricity and gas usage and costs for the current month, and the status of your Zonneplan home battery and EV charge point.
