LoJack

The LoJack integration connects Home Assistant to vehicles equipped with LoJack by Spireon GPS tracking hardware. After you authenticate with your Spireon/LoJack account credentials, the integration automatically discovers every vehicle registered to your account and creates a device for each one, with a device tracker entity that shows the vehicle's current or last known GPS location on the map.
