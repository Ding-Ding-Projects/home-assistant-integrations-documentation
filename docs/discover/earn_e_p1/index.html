EARN-E P1 Meter

The EARN-E P1 Meter integration connects to the EARN-E energy monitor, a device that reads your smart meter's P1 port and broadcasts real-time energy data via UDP on the local network. This integration listens for those broadcasts and exposes them as sensor entities.
