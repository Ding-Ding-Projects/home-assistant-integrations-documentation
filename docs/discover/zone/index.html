Zone

Turn places on a map into presence automations

Define home, work, school, or any other place as a zone, then use person and device tracker locations to detect arrivals and departures. Home Assistant creates a special 100 m home zone during onboarding; other zones can be placed and resized on the map, or configured in YAML.

From a place to a presence response

  1. Create a zone on the map
  2. Track a person or device
  3. Trigger an automation on arrival or departure

Inside the guide

  • Set a center point and radius for each place you want to track
  • Trigger automations when people or tracked devices enter or leave a zone
  • Mark a zone passive to keep it available for automations without showing it in the frontend or using it for device tracker state
