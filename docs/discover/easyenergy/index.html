easyEnergy

Follow energy prices through the day

easyEnergy connects Home Assistant to its cloud service for electricity and gas prices. It refreshes price data every ten minutes, with sensors for current and next-hour rates plus daily electricity comparisons.

Open the easyEnergy guide →

Read and request price data

  1. Confirm cloud access
  2. Refresh today's prices every 10 minutes
  3. Request price ranges with actions

Inside the guide

  • Setup checks the connection by requesting today's electricity prices before creating the easyEnergy entry.
  • The coordinator fetches today's electricity and gas data; after 14:00 UTC it also requests tomorrow's electricity prices.
  • Three response-only actions retrieve gas, electricity usage, or electricity return prices for a requested date range; electricity requests can use hourly or quarter-hour granularity.
