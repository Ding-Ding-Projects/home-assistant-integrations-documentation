Sofar

The Sofar integration connects Home Assistant to a Sofar Solar inverter over Modbus. You can reach the inverter over the network (Modbus TCP), either directly or through a Modbus TCP bridge, or over a serial port wired to its RS485 terminals (Modbus RTU).
