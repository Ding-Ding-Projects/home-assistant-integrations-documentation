Advantage Air

Bring Advantage Air climate control into Home Assistant

Connect an Advantage Air controller through its wall-mounted Android tablet to manage air conditioning systems and temperature-controlled zones. Home Assistant can also expose zone covers, sensors, and supported MyPlace lights, blinds, garage doors, and relays.

Open the Advantage Air guide →

From Advantage Air tablet to zone controls

  1. Assign a static IP to the tablet running MyPlace, e-zone, or zone10e
  2. Add Advantage Air in Home Assistant and enter the tablet's IP address
  3. Use climate entities for the system and temperature-controlled zones, with other entities available according to the controller and hardware

Inside the guide

  • Set a static IP for the tablet running MyPlace, e-zone, or zone10e, then enter it during setup
  • Use climate entities for the system and temperature-controlled zones; supported controllers may also expose covers, sensors, lights, blinds, garage doors, and relays
  • Adjust non-temperature-controlled zone covers in 5% increments and use advantage_air.set_time_to for time-to-on and time-to-off settings
