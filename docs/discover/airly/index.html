Airly

Put local air quality on your dashboard

Connect Home Assistant to Airly with an API key and a location to retrieve air quality readings from nearby measuring stations. Depending on what the closest station reports, sensors can include Common Air Quality Index (CAQI), particulate matter, gases, humidity, pressure, and temperature, with Airly's level, description, and advice available as CAQI attributes.
