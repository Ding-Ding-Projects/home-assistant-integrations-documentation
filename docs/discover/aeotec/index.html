Connect Aeotec devices through local radios

Use Home Assistant's Z-Wave and Zigbee integrations with Aeotec devices. Both device types work locally and require the matching Z-Wave or Zigbee stick; status updates and control happen locally in Home Assistant.

  2. Connect the matching Z-Wave or Zigbee stick
  3. Use the Z-Wave or Zigbee integration for local status and control
