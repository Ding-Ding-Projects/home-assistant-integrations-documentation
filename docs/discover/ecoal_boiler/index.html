eSterownik eCoal.pl Boiler

Read boiler temperatures and control its pumps

Connect an eSterownik eCoal.pl boiler controller to Home Assistant with its host address and login. The integration exposes controller temperature readings as Celsius sensors and offers switches for supported heating and hot water pumps.

Open the eSterownik eCoal.pl Boiler guide →

From controller connection to boiler controls

  1. Provide the controller host and credentials
  2. Home Assistant checks the controller status
  3. Read temperature sensors and control available pump switches

Inside the guide

  • Configure the controller host; the integration defaults the username and password to admin
  • Read up to ten temperature values, including indoor, outdoor, hot water, feedwater, fuel feeder, and exhaust readings
  • Monitor and switch the central heating, second central heating, and domestic hot water pumps
