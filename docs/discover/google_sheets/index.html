Google Sheets

The Google Sheets integration allows you to connect your Google Drive to Home Assistant. The integration adds an action to allow you to append rows to a Sheets document. The idea is that you can store data on there for further processing. When you set up a config entry, your drive will have a new sheet called Home Assistant. You can then rename this to whatever you like.
