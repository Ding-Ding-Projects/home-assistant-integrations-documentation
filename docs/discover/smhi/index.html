SMHI

The SMHI integration brings weather forecasts and fire risk forecasts from SMHI, the Swedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute, into Home Assistant. Use it to show the weather and forecasts for your home on your dashboard, or to get notified when thunder or a high fire risk is expected.
