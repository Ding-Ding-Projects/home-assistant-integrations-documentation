The Energy Detective TED5000

The The Energy Detective TED5000 integration monitors electricity consumption/production by connecting to the TED gateway, itself connected to one or several Measuring Transmitting Units (MTU). The platform creates up to two sensors per MTU, one for Wattage the other for Voltage.
