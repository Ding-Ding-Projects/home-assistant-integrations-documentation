PrusaLink

The PrusaLink integration allows you to monitor your Prusa 3D printer and its progress with your Home Assistant installation. This integration works with Prusa MINI/MINI+, Prusa MK3.9/MK4, Prusa XL, Prusa CORE One, and with the older Raspberry Pi-based Prusa MK2.5/MK3.
