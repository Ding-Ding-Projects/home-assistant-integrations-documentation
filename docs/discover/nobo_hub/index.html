Nobø Ecohub

The Nobø Ecohub integration connects Home Assistant to your Nobø Ecohub, a Norwegian heating controller from Glen Dimplex Nordic AS. The hub manages electric panel heaters, floor heating, and on/off receivers grouped into zones, and lets you follow weekly heating schedules with eco, comfort, and away setpoints.
