Novy Cooker Hood

The Novy Cooker Hood integration lets you control the light and extractor of a Novy cooker hood over 433.92&nbsp;MHz radio, through a compatible RF transmitter. You can automatically run the extractor when the hob is in use, for example at 50%, as soon as a smart plug reports power draw, and switch the light by voice through Assist, which is handy when your hands are full in the kitchen.
