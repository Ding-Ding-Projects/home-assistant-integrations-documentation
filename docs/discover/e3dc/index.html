E3/DC

Follow power through your E3/DC system

Connect an E3/DC home power station to Home Assistant over local Modbus TCP. Once configured, the integration polls the station every five seconds for live power-flow and battery readings.

Open the E3/DC guide →

From local connection to power readings

  1. Enable Modbus TCP with E3/DC protocol in the Smart Home menu
  2. Discover the station or enter its host and Modbus port
  3. Poll power flows, battery readings, and available status sensors

Inside the guide

  • Track PV, house, grid, and battery power alongside battery charge, self-consumption, and self-sufficiency readings
  • See status signals for charging locks, forecast-limited charging, feed-in limits, and emergency-power readiness
  • Wallbox power sensors appear when a wallbox is configured; additional-feeder power appears when a supported external meter is present
