Acomax

Bring Acomax devices into Home Assistant

Acomax devices are supported through the Motion Blinds integration. The brand's devices are rebranded products or share a communication protocol with Motion Blinds, so you can add Acomax in Home Assistant and follow the supporting integration's documentation for use.

Set up Acomax support

  1. Open Settings > Devices & services in Home Assistant
  2. Select Acomax and follow the on-screen instructions
  3. Refer to Motion Blinds documentation for usage information

Inside the guide

  • Add Acomax from Settings > Devices & services and follow the on-screen setup
  • Use the Motion Blinds integration for Acomax device support and usage guidance
