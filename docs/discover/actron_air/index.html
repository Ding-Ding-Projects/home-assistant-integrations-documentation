Actron Air

Bring Actron Air zones into Home Assistant

Connect an Actron Air Neo or Que controller to Home Assistant to manage the main air conditioning system and its zones. Climate entities expose temperature, operation mode, and fan speed where supported, while sensors report system and connected peripheral data.

Open the Actron Air guide →

From Actron Air controller to zone controls

  1. Prepare a Neo or Que controller with internet access and an email registration
  2. Add the Actron Air integration in Home Assistant
  3. Use climate entities for the system and zones, and monitor available sensors and switches

Inside the guide

  • Requires an Actron Air air conditioner with a Neo or Que controller, an active internet connection, and registration to an email address
  • Use climate entities for the main system and zones, plus sensors for filter status, temperatures, dampers, and connected peripherals where supported
  • Use read-only zone cover reporting and supported switches for Away, Continuous fan, Quiet, and Turbo modes
