HomeLink

The HomeLink integration allows you to integrate your HomeLink devices in Home Assistant. The HomeLink app allows HomeLink owners to trigger smart home routines in a variety of cloud-based home automation platforms from the comfort of their vehicle. When you arrive home, one HomeLink button press can adjust the thermostat, turn on lights, disarm the security system, unlock the door, and begin playing your favorite music.
