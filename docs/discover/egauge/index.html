eGauge

The eGauge integration is used to integrate with eGauge energy monitors. eGauge provides energy monitors for residential and commercial applications. They are commonly used with solar energy installations. The eGauge integration can expose sensor readings from eGauge devices into Home Assistant, including energy meters that work with the Energy Dashboard.
