OVHcloud AI Endpoints

The OVHcloud AI Endpoints integration adds a conversation agent powered by OVHcloud AI Endpoints to Home Assistant. OVHcloud AI Endpoints is a serverless inference platform that gives you access to a curated catalog of open-weight large language models hosted in Europe.
