Nettigo Air Monitor

The Nettigo Air Monitor integration allows you to read temperature, humidity, pressure, and air quality data from Nettigo Air Monitor devices. Nettigo Air Monitor is a DIY air quality monitoring system with open source firmware, based on an open hardware project.
