Queensland Bushfire Alert

Instructions on how to integrate the Queensland Bushfire Alert feed into Home Assistant.

Overview

The Queensland Bushfire Alert integration lets you integrate a GeoRSS feed containing bushfire alerts for Queensland. It retrieves alerts from a feed and shows information of those alerts filtered by distance to Home Assistant's location.
