MELCloud Home

Instructions on how to integrate MELCloud Home with Home Assistant.

Overview

The MELCloud Home integration connects Home Assistant to MELCloud Home, Mitsubishi Electric's cloud service for managing their air conditioning and heat pump products.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for MELCloud Home.

The reauth confirm configuration step lists email, password.

The reconfigure configuration step lists email, password.
