WaterFurnace

Instructions on how to integrate WaterFurnace Geothermal System into Home Assistant.

Overview

The WaterFurnace integration communicates with the WaterFurnace Symphony website's WebSocket to show you many of the sensors in your system. While not an official API, this is the same backend the Symphony website is based on, and should be reasonably stable.

Climate

The integration creates a climate entity for each geothermal system found in your account. You can use this entity to set the target temperature, humidity setpoint, and HVAC mode directly from Home Assistant.

Energy

The integration is also able to track historic energy use. You can track the total energy consumption in the energy dashboard. This data is refreshed every 2 hours, so your energy use may lag behind.

Prerequisites

To use Waterfurnace in your installation, you need to configure the integration with your Symphony Waterfurnace account.

Limitations

If your account has more than one location, only devices in the first location will be available.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for WaterFurnace.

The reauth confirm configuration step lists password, username.

The user configuration step lists password, username.
