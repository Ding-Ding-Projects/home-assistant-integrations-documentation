Swisscom Internet-Box

Instructions on how to integrate the Swisscom Internet-Box into Home Assistant.

Overview

The Swisscom Internet-Box integration offers presence detection by looking at the devices connected to your Internet-Box router. The Internet-Box is the router provided by Swisscom, an Internet provider in Switzerland.

Data updates

Home Assistant polling polls your Internet-Box every 30 seconds to retrieve the list of connected devices and update their connection status.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for Swisscom Internet-Box.
