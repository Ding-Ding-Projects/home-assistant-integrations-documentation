Google Health

Instructions on how to integrate Google Health within Home Assistant.

Overview

The Google Health integration allows you to expose health and fitness data from Google Health (including Fitbit, Pixel Watch, and other devices connected to your Google Account) to Home Assistant.

Prerequisites

If your Google Account is enrolled in the Advanced Protection Program, you cannot use the setup described below. Advanced Protection blocks unverified apps from accessing sensitive account data, including your Google Health data. Publishing your OAuth app does not verify it.

Google Health automation examples

The real power of this integration is automating your home environment or notifications based on your health metrics. Here are a few ideas to get you started.

Automation: Step goal reminder

Send a notification in the evening if you have not met your daily step goal.

Automation: Resting heart rate alert

Notify you if your daily resting heart rate goes above a certain threshold (for example, 80 bpm), which could indicate fatigue or stress.

Symptom: Integration doesn't work as expected

The Google Health integration was set up with incorrect settings, for example, the wrong client ID or client secret, and does not work as expected.

Description

Home Assistant stores the credentials you entered during the initial setup as application credentials. Setting up the integration again reuses these stored credentials, so the incorrect configuration persists until you remove them.

Resolution

1. Delete the credentials in the Application Credentials user interface. 2. Set up the integration again.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for Google Health.
