SenseCAP

Monitor SenseCAP sensors over LoRaWAN.

Overview

The SenseCAP integration adds Seeed Studio SenseCAP sensors from your LoRaWAN connections.

Supported devices

Every model also has a Battery sensor, shown under Diagnostic on the device page. S2106 and S2107 require ChirpStack catalog identities.

Prerequisites

Set up a LoRaWAN connection integration, such as ChirpStack, and select the applications containing your sensors. The connection must be available and include at least one supported sensor before you can add SenseCAP.

Data updates

Readings update when a device sends an uplink. Sensors show Unknown until their first reading after Home Assistant starts. If a LoRaWAN connection is lost, its sensors become unavailable until the connection recovers.

Readings are unknown or unavailable

For Unknown readings, check that uplinks appear in your LoRaWAN server and wait for the device's next transmission. For Unavailable sensors, check the connection integration and its credentials.

Removing the integration

Removing SenseCAP removes its devices and entities from Home Assistant. Your LoRaWAN connections remain configured.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for SenseCAP.
