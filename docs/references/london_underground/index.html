London Underground

Display the current status of London underground & overground lines within Home Assistant.

Overview

The London Underground integration will display the status of London underground lines, as well as the overground lines and the DLR.

Automation example:

This automation triggers when the status of the Victoria line changes to something significant, and just before commutes are likely to begin. To avoid spam, it only runs if the commuter is at home in the morning or away from home in the evening.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for London Underground.

The user configuration step lists line.
