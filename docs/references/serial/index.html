Raw Serial

Instructions on how to integrate data from serial connected sensors into Home Assistant.

Overview

The Raw Serial integration reads raw text from a serial port. To get useful values, you usually need to process that text with templates. Most devices with a serial port already have an integration that handles the connection for you. Before you use this integration, search the integrations for your device or its brand.

Raw Serial sensor

The Raw Serial sensor reads the raw data from a serial port and makes it available as a sensor entity. Use it when no integration is available for your device.

Finding the device path of a serial port

If a serial port is detected by Home Assistant but is used only by serial sensors configured in your configuration.yaml, or only by Modbus, it is not tracked as having a consumer. As a result, it appears in the Available section of the panel rather than the Connected section.

Troubleshooting

For problems with the serial connection itself, such as a port that is not listed or a device that does not respond, refer to Troubleshooting serial connections.

The domain used to identify Raw Serial in Home Assistant is serial.
