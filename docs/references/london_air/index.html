London Air

Display the current status of London air quality by area and pollution type.

Overview

The London Air integration queries the London air quality data feed provided by Kings College London. A single sensor will be added for each location (local authority district or borough) specified in the configuration file. The state of each sensor is the overall air quality in that borough. Note that only 28 of the 32 boroughs have data available.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for London Air.
