U.S. Geological Survey Earthquake Hazards (USGS)

Instructions on how to integrate the U.S. Geological Survey Earthquake Hazards Program Feed feed into Home Assistant.

Overview

The U.S. Geological Survey Earthquake Hazards (USGS) integration lets you integrate a GeoJSON feed provided by the U.S. Geological Survey with information about seismic events like earthquakes. It retrieves incidents from a feed and shows information of those incidents filtered by distance to Home Assistant's location.
