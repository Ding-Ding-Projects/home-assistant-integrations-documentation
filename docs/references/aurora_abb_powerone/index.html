Aurora ABB PowerOne Solar PV

Instructions on how to integrate an Aurora ABB PowerOne solar inverter within Home Assistant.

Overview

This integration implements a direct RS485 connection to a solar inverter in the PVI-3.0/3.6/4.2-TL-OUTD ABB series, and may work on others. The inverter was formerly made by PowerOne who got taken over by ABB.

Configuration details

In Home Assistant, open Settings → Devices & services, choose Add integration, and search for Aurora ABB PowerOne Solar PV.

The user configuration step lists address, port.
