Yoto

Instructions on how to integrate Yoto players with Home Assistant.

Overview

The Yoto integration lets you control your Yoto audio players from Home Assistant. You can play and pause cards, change the volume, skip tracks, seek within a track, see what is currently playing, and browse your card library to start a specific card, chapter, or track. You can also monitor each player's battery level, what content source it is playing from, and its current day or night mode.

The pick implementation configuration step lists implementation.
