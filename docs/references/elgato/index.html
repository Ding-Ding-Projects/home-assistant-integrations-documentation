Elgato Light

Instructions on how to integrate an Elgato Light with Home Assistant.

Overview

The Elgato Light integration lets you control Elgato LED lighting devices locally over your network. Elgato Lights are designed for streamers, content creators, and home studio setups, and are commonly used on platforms like YouTube and Twitch.

Prerequisites

Your Elgato Light must be powered on and connected to your local network. In most cases, the integration automatically discovers the device on your network.
