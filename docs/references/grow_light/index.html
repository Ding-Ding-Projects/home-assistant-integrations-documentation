Grow light

Grow light

A helper that keeps a plug on for a set number of hours each day and switches it off while a door is open. It can also follow a crop: pick one of the catalogue's vegetables or legumes and the light hours and watering follow the crop's growth stage, counted from the day it was sown.

Add it from Settings > Devices & services > Helpers > Create helper > Grow light. Boards: design/canvases/43-grow-light.

Settings

Setting What it does
Name The name of the grow light and its entities.
Plug or switch The switch, light or input_boolean that powers the light.
Crop Optional. A searchable list of the crop catalogue, each row with a photo of the plant, its group, day-length type, days to the end and the photo credit. "None, set my own hours" keeps the fixed hours below.
Sown or planted on The day the growth stage is counted from. Defaults to today.
Start time When the light turns on each day. Watering is due at this time too.
Hours per day Used only without a crop. 0.5 to 24 hours in half-hour steps. A window that passes midnight works, for example 20:00 for 8 hours ends at 04:00. 24 means always on.
Pause when open Optional door or window sensors (binary_sensor).
Water every Days between waterings without a crop; 0 means no watering schedule. With a crop the stage sets it.
Watering pump or valve Optional switch or input_boolean. When set, the helper waters by itself when watering is due.
Watering run How many minutes the pump or valve runs each time.
Soil moisture sensor Optional sensor with the moisture device class. Scheduled watering is skipped while the soil reads wet, and soil going dry starts watering or a reminder.
Water when soil is below The dry threshold in percent for the soil moisture sensor. Default 30.
Send alerts to Optional notify actions, for example notify.mobile_app_phone. Any notify action can be typed in.
Sow a new batch every Optional days between sowings for batch crops such as radish, lettuce or mung beans. 0 means no reminder.
Light power Optional rated watts of the light, used to count its energy.
Plug power sensor Optional sensor with the power device class. When set, the energy count uses its readings instead of the light power.
Electricity price Optional price per kWh in the instance's currency. 0 hides the cost sensor.

All of them except the name can be changed later from the helper's Options; the helper reloads with the new settings.

How it behaves

  • Inside the on hours, with every door closed, the plug is turned on.
  • When the on hours end, the plug is turned off.
  • When any listed door opens (its state is on), the plug is turned off at once.
  • When the last open door closes, the plug is turned back on only if it is still inside the on hours. Closing a door outside the on hours leaves the light off.
  • With a crop, the on hours each day are the light hours of the stage the crop is in on the day the window starts. Germination usually has 0 hours, so the light stays off until the seedlings are due; the next window start after a stage change brings the new hours. After the last stage the crop stays in it.
  • Watering is due at the start time, every so many days after the last watering (the first one on the sowing day). With a pump or valve, the helper turns it on for the watering run and records the watering. Without one, press Watered after watering by hand. Watering ignores the doors and stops while the schedule switch is off.
  • The helper only switches the plug when something changes: the on hours start or end, a door opens or closes, the schedule switch changes, Home Assistant starts, or the plug comes back online after being unavailable. Switching the plug by hand is left alone until the next change.
  • A door that is unavailable or unknown counts as closed.
  • With a soil moisture sensor, a watering that falls due while the soil reads at or above the threshold is put off to the same time the next day. When the reading drops below the threshold, the pump runs at once (or a "soil dry" reminder is sent). A dry reading within 6 hours of a watering is ignored, since the water may still be soaking in.
  • Alerts are sent for a new growth stage, the harvest stage, watering due (only without a pump, and once per due time), a pump watering run, dry soil and the sowing reminder. Each one fires the grow_light_alert event (name, kind, message), updates the Alerts event entity and goes to every notify action chosen. Stage alerts are only sent for changes after Home Assistant started, so a restart does not repeat them.
  • Energy is counted while the plug is on, from the plug's power sensor or else the light power, and is kept across restarts.

Entities

Entity What it shows
Switch Schedule Turns the helper on or off. Turning it off switches the plug off and stops all automatic switching and watering until it is turned on again. Its state is kept across restarts.
Sensor Status on, paused (a door is open), off_hours or schedule_off. The open_doors attribute lists the doors open now.
Sensor Next change When the on hours next start or end. Empty while the schedule is off or when the light is on all day or not at all.
Sensor Growth stage Only with a crop. The stage, with the crop's photo as its picture, and attributes for the day in the stage, days since sowing, today's light hours, watering interval and note, and every stage of the crop.
Sensor Next watering When watering is next due; empty without a watering schedule. watering is true while the pump runs.
Sensor Last watered The last watering, by hand or by the pump. Kept across restarts.
Button Watered Records a watering done by hand.
Button Sown again Only with a crop. Sets the sowing date to today and reloads, so the stages start over.
Event Alerts The latest alert, with its message. Event types: stage_changed, ready_to_harvest, water_due, watered, soil_dry, sow_next.
Sensor Next sowing Only with a crop and a sowing interval. When the next batch is due.
Sensor Light energy Only with a light power or power sensor. kWh used so far; can go on the Energy dashboard.
Sensor Light cost Only with energy and a price. The energy times the price.

The Next watering sensor also carries soil_moisture and dry_below when a soil moisture sensor is set.

Cards

  • Grow light card (type: grow-light-card, entity: the grow light's Status sensor): the crop photo, stage with a stage bar, light status, light hours, next change, watering, soil moisture, energy, cost, next sowing, and the Watered and Sown again buttons where those entities exist. Without a crop it shows the light parts only.
  • Grow light overview card (type: grow-light-overview-card, optional title): every grow light side by side, one column on a phone.

Both are in the card picker. Not yet seen in a browser.

Crop catalogue

The catalogue is core/homeassistant/components/grow_light/plants.json, with one photo per crop in photos/ and the photo credits in photos/CREDITS.md. The photos are bundled and served by Home Assistant itself at /grow_light/photos/<crop>.jpg, so the picker works without internet access.

Read the numbers as starting points. Day-length types and total days to harvest come from the sources listed for each crop (mostly RHS grow-your-own pages, Wikipedia and Purdue NewCrop; several university extension pages could not be read). The split into stages, the light hours for each stage and the watering intervals are practical indoor-growing estimates for pots under a grow light; the sources give watering only as general advice such as "keep the soil moist". Adjust to your pots, soil and room: a crop can be replaced with "None, set my own hours" at any time.

Known weak spots: celery and parsnip are shown by their wild forms, collard greens by Portuguese kale and rutabaga by a field of swede; the pepper entries borrow their stage pattern from aubergine; asparagus is modelled on its first year only; onions differ by long-day, intermediate and short-day variety, which the note explains.

# Grow light

A helper that keeps a plug on for a set number of hours each day and switches it off while a door
is open. It can also follow a crop: pick one of the catalogue's vegetables or legumes and the light
hours and watering follow the crop's growth stage, counted from the day it was sown.

Add it from **Settings > Devices & services > Helpers > Create helper > Grow light**. Boards:
[`design/canvases/43-grow-light`](../../../design/canvases/43-grow-light/README.md).

with the new settings.

## How it behaves

- Inside the on hours, with every door closed, the plug is turned on.
- When the on hours end, the plug is turned off.
- When any listed door opens (its state is `on`), the plug is turned off at once.
- When the last open door closes, the plug is turned back on **only if it is still inside the on
  hours**. Closing a door outside the on hours leaves the light off.
- With a crop, the on hours each day are the light hours of the stage the crop is in on the day the
  window starts. Germination usually has 0 hours, so the light stays off until the seedlings are
  due; the next window start after a stage change brings the new hours. After the last stage the
  crop stays in it.
- Watering is due at the start time, every so many days after the last watering (the first one on
  the sowing day). With a pump or valve, the helper turns it on for the watering run and records
  the watering. Without one, press **Watered** after watering by hand. Watering ignores the doors
  and stops while the schedule switch is off.
- The helper only switches the plug when something changes: the on hours start or end, a door
  opens or closes, the schedule switch changes, Home Assistant starts, or the plug comes back
  online after being unavailable. Switching the plug by hand is left alone until the next change.
- A door that is `unavailable` or `unknown` counts as closed.
- With a soil moisture sensor, a watering that falls due while the soil reads at or above the
  threshold is put off to the same time the next day. When the reading drops below the threshold,
  the pump runs at once (or a "soil dry" reminder is sent). A dry reading within 6 hours of a
  watering is ignored, since the water may still be soaking in.
- Alerts are sent for a new growth stage, the harvest stage, watering due (only without a pump, and
  once per due time), a pump watering run, dry soil and the sowing reminder. Each one fires the
  `grow_light_alert` event (`name`, `kind`, `message`), updates the **Alerts** event entity and goes
  to every notify action chosen. Stage alerts are only sent for changes after Home Assistant
  started, so a restart does not repeat them.
- Energy is counted while the plug is on, from the plug's power sensor or else the light power, and
  is kept across restarts.

## Crop catalogue

The catalogue is `core/homeassistant/components/grow_light/plants.json`, with one photo per crop in
`photos/` and the photo credits in `photos/CREDITS.md`. The photos are bundled and served by Home
Assistant itself at `/grow_light/photos/<crop>.jpg`, so the picker works without internet access.

**Read the numbers as starting points.** Day-length types and total days to harvest come from the
sources listed for each crop (mostly RHS grow-your-own pages, Wikipedia and Purdue NewCrop; several
university extension pages could not be read). The split into stages, the light hours for each
stage and the watering intervals are practical indoor-growing estimates for pots under a grow
light; the sources give watering only as general advice such as "keep the soil moist". Adjust to
your pots, soil and room: a crop can be replaced with "None, set my own hours" at any time.

Known weak spots: celery and parsnip are shown by their wild forms, collard greens by Portuguese
kale and rutabaga by a field of swede; the pepper entries borrow their stage pattern from aubergine;
asparagus is modelled on its first year only; onions differ by long-day, intermediate and short-day
variety, which the note explains.

- `tests/components/grow_light/test_schedule.py`: the on-hours window, including across midnight.
- `tests/components/grow_light/test_plants.py`: stage counting from the sowing day, every catalogue
  entry valid with a bundled photo, and the crops grown at home present.
- `tests/components/grow_light/test_init.py`, `test_crop.py`, `test_config_flow.py`: start-up, door
  pause and resume, door closed outside the hours, end of the window, schedule switch off,
  germination keeping the light off, seedling hours, the stage change turning the light on, the
  pump watering when due and stopping after the run, the Watered button, own watering days, and the
  setup step. Run on Linux; see `ROADMAP.md` for the latest result.
- `tests/components/grow_light/test_alerts.py`: the stage change alert to a notify action, one
  watering reminder per due time, wet soil putting the pump off and dry soil starting it, the dry
  soil reminder without a pump, energy and cost from the light power, no energy sensor without a
  power source, the sowing reminder, and the Sown again button.
