De Lijn

Instructions on how to integrate De Lijn (Flemish public transport company) departure times into Home Assistant.

Overview

The De Lijn integration will give you the departure time of the next bus, tram or subway at a specific stop of the De Lijn public transport network in Flanders (Belgium).

Setup

Create a developer account at De Lijn Open Data portal to get a free API subscription key. For valid stop IDs check for the 6 digits at the physical stops or visit the stops page of the De Lijn website.
