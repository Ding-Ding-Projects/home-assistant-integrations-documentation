Using Brunt Blind Engine

Overview

The Brunt Blind Engine integration allows one to control Blind Engines by Brunt. To use it, you need a Brunt App Account. All Brunt Blind devices registered to your account are automatically added to your Home Assistant with the names given them through the Brunt app.
