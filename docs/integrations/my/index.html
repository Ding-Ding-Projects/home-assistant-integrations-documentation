    Using My Home Assistant

    Overview

    The My Home Assistant integration adds support for the My Home Assistant redirect service. The service lets a link from outside Home Assistant, such as a link in this documentation, open a specific page inside your own Home Assistant instance without needing to know its hostname or IP address.

    How it works

    My Home Assistant is a redirect service hosted at my.home-assistant.io. When you select a My link, the service asks your browser which Home Assistant instance you want to open. Your instance URL is stored locally in your browser and is never sent to any external service, not even to the My Home Assistant service itself. Once you have chosen your instance, subsequent My links open directly without asking again.

    Creating your own My links

    You can create your own My links to share with others. Visit my.home-assistant.io/create-link to browse the available redirects and generate a link you can copy and share.
