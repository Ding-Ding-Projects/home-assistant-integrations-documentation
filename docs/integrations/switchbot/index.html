Using SwitchBot Bluetooth

Works with Home Assistant certified devices

SwitchBot Matter devices are certified for use via one of their Matter hubs: either the SwitchBot Hub 2 or the Hub 3. Some are also certified via Matter over Wi-Fi as standalone devices.
