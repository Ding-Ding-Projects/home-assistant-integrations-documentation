Using EnergyID

Overview

The EnergyID integration connects your Home Assistant to EnergyID—a cloud platform for energy monitoring and optimization. This integration uploads your Home Assistant sensor data and provides advanced analytics and performance tracking for solar, battery, energy consumption, and more.
