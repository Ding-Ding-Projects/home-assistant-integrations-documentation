Using Fing

Overview

The Fing integration allows Home Assistant to retrieve detailed information about the devices on your local network. Fing helps home users and IT professionals monitor, protect, and understand their networks with intuitive tools that make connectivity management simple and effective. By connecting through the Local API, Fing automatically builds and synchronizes an inventory of devices to detect online presence and trigger automations.
