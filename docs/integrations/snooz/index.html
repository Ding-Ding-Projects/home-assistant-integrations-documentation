Using Snooz

Overview

Integrates SNOOZ devices into Home Assistant.

Setup

Once a device is discovered, it needs to be put into pairing mode to complete setup.

Entering pairing mode

1. Ensure the device is not connected to a mobile app or any other Bluetooth controller. 2. Hold your finger on the power button until all buttons begin pulsing on and off (~ 5 seconds).

Fan

Devices are exposed as Fan entities with a persistent connection to the device.
