Using Blue Current

Overview

The Blue Current integration allows you to connect to your blue current account to Home Assistant and monitor your charge point(s).

Prerequisites

1. Log in to my.bluecurrent.nl. 2. Click on your username and go to settings. 3. Enable advanced options. 4. Click on your username again and go to advanced. 5. Generate an API token.
